Durable, precisely targeted medicines that help people keep their sight.

The need

Vision loss is rising as populations age

Age-related macular degeneration and diabetic eye disease are among the main causes of vision loss. Today’s biologic treatments are injected into the eye, often every one to four months, and many disease pathways still have no treatment at all.

Our approach

01 · The problem

Key disease targets are out of reach

Many proteins behind retinal disease act through large, flat contact surfaces that small molecules can’t grip. Biologics reach some of them, but need injections into the eye every few weeks or months.

02 · Our approach

Peptides designed for the eye

We design peptides that bind these surfaces with antibody-like precision, in a much smaller molecule built for ocular delivery.

How the platform works

  1. Step 1 · Design

    Design binders in silico

    Structure-based and machine-learning models generate peptide candidates for a chosen protein interface.

  2. Step 2 · Test

    Make and screen

    Candidates are synthesized and tested for binding, selectivity and stability with partner labs.

  3. Step 3 · Optimize

    Optimize for the eye

    We tune size, solubility and stability for delivery to the retina and a longer duration of action.

About these diseases

Wet AMD

Leaky blood vessels under the retina damage central vision. Treatment means repeated injections into the eye.

Dry AMD and geographic atrophy

Cells in the macula slowly die off. Current treatments slow the damage but do not stop it.
