Our approach

01 · The problem

Key disease targets are out of reach

Many proteins behind retinal disease act through large, flat contact surfaces that small molecules can’t grip. Biologics reach some of them, but need injections into the eye every few weeks or months.

02 · Our approach

Peptides designed for the eye

We design peptides that bind these surfaces with antibody-like precision, in a much smaller molecule built for ocular delivery.

03 · How we work

Design, test, optimize

We design candidates computationally, test them with partner labs, and optimize the best ones for stability and delivery in the eye.

Why peptides

The right size for flat protein surfaces

Focus

Diseases that take central vision

We are starting with degenerative diseases of the retina. Age-related macular degeneration is a leading cause of severe visual impairment and blindness in the United States,2 and diabetic eye disease is the leading cause of vision loss among working-age adults in the US.3

196Mpeople worldwide were living with age-related macular degeneration in 20201
288Mare projected to be living with it by 20401

Wet AMD

Leaky blood vessels under the retina damage central vision. Treatment means repeated injections into the eye.

Dry AMD and geographic atrophy

Cells in the macula slowly die off. Current treatments slow the damage but do not stop it.

Diabetic eye disease

Damaged retinal vessels leak fluid into the macula and blur central vision.
